How TrustBridge Works

1
Negotiate

Two autonomous AI agents debate buyer requirements vs developer rates over multi-round deliberation until reaching consensus.

POST /api/v1/negotiate
2
Escrow

Agreed terms auto-generate a structured contract. The buyer funds the escrow vault via PayPal sandbox authorization.

POST /contracts/{id}/fund
3
Verify

The developer submits deliverables (PR URL, commit SHA, test telemetry). The AI verification engine evaluates criteria satisfaction.

POST /milestones/{id}/submit
4
Release

Upon verified acceptance criteria, programmatic release executes payout to developer with guaranteed locked exchange rate.

POST /milestones/{id}/release
