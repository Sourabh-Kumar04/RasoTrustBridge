Why TrustBridge Replaces Traditional Freelance Platforms

Legacy Platforms (Upwork / Fiverr / Direct Wire)
  • Net-60 payment delays and arbitrary platform freezes on freelancer accounts.
  • Currency depreciation risk: 10-15% annualized swings in emerging market currencies erode freelancer margins.
  • Subjective dispute arbitration where human reviewers lack technical context to judge code quality.
  • 20% fee extraction with zero programmable escrow or automated milestone verification.
The TrustBridge Protocol
  • Programmatic atomic release: Funds disburse automatically upon satisfying objective acceptance criteria.
  • Locked-at-signing FX hedge: Developer receives the exact agreed INR/EUR amount, insulated from dollar fluctuations.
  • Objective cryptographic proof: Pytest suites and GitHub PR diffs evaluated by deterministic AI verifier.
  • Bilateral agent consensus: AI buyer and seller negotiate bounds and agree before anyone locks money.

Funds never enter a personal checking account or an unregulated pool. They sit in an isolated PayPal dual-custody escrow order authorized under the TrustBridge protocol.

Can the buyer take the money back arbitrarily?

No. Once escrow is funded, the buyer cannot cancel or withdraw unilaterally. Funds can only be released upon satisfying agreed milestone verification criteria, or refunded if work fails objective tests.

Can the freelancer take the money without delivering?

No. Payouts are programmatic. The dual-custody vault will not release a single dollar until the developer submits code that passes automated test suites and cryptographic PR diff verification.

What if the local currency crashes while work is underway?

Zero currency loss. Your payout rate is locked at signing. TrustBridge locks the foreign exchange rate so the developer receives the exact agreed payout in their local currency (INR, EUR, GBP) regardless of dollar market volatility.

What happens if a dispute is filed?

The dual-custody vault freezes immediately. Neither party can withdraw. Both parties enter a 72-hour mediation window with access to immutable commit logs and test telemetry.
